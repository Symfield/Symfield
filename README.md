# Symfield PBC

**Mathematics for thresholds, transitions, and emergence.**

Symfield PBC develops mathematical frameworks and architectures for understanding threshold states, system transitions, and emergent behavior in complex systems. Its research in non-collapse architectures examines how mathematical structures can encode relationships that survive observation.

Its central concern is how relationships and continuity can be maintained as systems change, interact, and are observed. This guides a research and development program in non-collapse architectures, spanning mathematical foundations, computational models, and analytical instruments.

Methodologically, Symfield begins with what a system does, then develops or selects the mathematics, scale, representation, and measures adequate to the behavior under study, rather than selecting the formalism in advance and treating its representational limits as limits of the system.

The ambition is to build systems that preserve consequential relationships through transition. The work makes that ambition testable through explicit assumptions, formal results, and computational investigation.

Founded by Nicole Flynn, Symfield is a Delaware public benefit corporation.

Research, papers, and essays: [symfield.ai](https://www.symfield.ai/)

## Research focus

- Mathematical structures: tolerance-modified exactness, geometric opposition, and drift diagnostics.
- Transitions and boundaries: how formation conditions, coupling, and prior states shape subsequent behavior.
- Measurement and description: what an apparatus makes accessible, what its representations preserve, and where their limits arise.
- Computational architectures: models and analytical instruments for investigating these questions.
